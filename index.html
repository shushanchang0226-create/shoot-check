Driving speed

Light is rated from the forecast cloud cover and the sun height. Dim: sun below 15°, or cloud 90% or more with the sun below 30°. Overcast: cloud at or above your overcast setting (%). Bright: everything else. Motion during each frame is driving speed × shutter time. The target is less than 2 cm, so fine detail stays sharp for 3D work. Tap an hour above, in the grid or in the timeline to see its settings.

How the verdict works

Each hour is checked on five items. All five green means Go. Any one red means No go. Everything else is Maybe. When the sun is below ° it is too dark, shown in grey.

Wind and gusts are forecast at 10 m above the ground. At camera height (about 2 m) the wind is usually lower, so these limits are on the safe side. Below 20 km/h (up to a gentle breeze) leaves and small twigs move but branches stay still. From 20 to 30 km/h small branches move, so check the footage. Above 30 km/h small trees sway and gusts can shake the camera mount. Gust limits are set higher because gusts are always stronger than the mean wind.
